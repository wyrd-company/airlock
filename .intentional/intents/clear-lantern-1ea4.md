Add the terminal interface's finding detail and policy inspector. The detail reads one rule whole — its evidence, its error with the two 403s held apart at error.cause, the authorization behind a suppression, the remediation it retains under one, why the rule applies, and what its status at its severity does to complete and conformant. The inspector shows the effective policy: every rule with its severity, evaluation mode, and provenance, the registry digest in full with what it proves and what it excludes, where the rules came from including policy-sourced suppressions and conditionally enabled rules, and the run's provenance. Readings longer than the terminal scroll rather than being shortened, and both screens can offer their identifying value to the terminal's clipboard.
